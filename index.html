The Inspection Checklist

Everything We Verify Inside and Outside the Apartment

Pillar 1

Inside the Apartment (Technical & Bills)

  • Window insulation (doppi vetri vs. single pane, drafts, shutters/tapparelle, and street noise).
  • Heating system (riscaldamento centralizzato vs. autonomo), electricity, gas, and spese condominiali breakdown.
  • Balcony presence, usable appliances (washing machine, oven, fridge space), water pressure, dampness/mold check, and overall condition.
Pillar 2

Roommates & Flat Dynamic

  • Exact count of bedrooms and bathrooms (verifying person-to-bathroom ratio).
  • Brief, respectful profile of current flatmates (students vs. workers, languages spoken, cleanliness of common areas, and overall house atmosphere).
Pillar 3

Turin Neighborhood & Transit Guide

  • Exact walking and public transit time to your specific university campus.
  • Nearby daily essentials (supermarkets, pharmacies, study cafes, and safe evening transit stops).
  • Custom orientation guide showing which GTT buses, trams, or Metro lines connect you to Porta Nuova, Porta Susa, Piazza Castello, and Turin's key landmarks.
Pillar 4

Contract Preparation Guidance (Advisory Only)

  • Step-by-step checklist of documents needed before signing (Codice Fiscale, Passport/ID, university enrollment proof, guarantor/garante paperwork) and deposit safety tips.
We guide you on preparation; we do not draft, legally review, or sign contracts.

Terms of Service (Termini e Condizioni di Servizio)

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Nature of Service — Strictly NOT a Real Estate Agency

RoomChecked Torino ("we," "us," or "our") operates exclusively as an independent on-site apartment viewing, visual inspection, and local orientation assistant for students relocating to Turin, Italy.

  • No Brokerage (Nessuna Mediazione Immobiliare): RoomChecked Torino is NOT a licensed real estate agency (agenzia immobiliare) pursuant to Italian Law 39/1989. We do not source proprietary listings, match landlords with tenants, negotiate lease terms, hold security deposits (caparre), or draft, verify, or sign rental contracts.
  • Client-Sourced Listings: All property links and landlord contacts must be independently found and provided by the Client. Our fee compensates strictly for physical travel, visual/technical inspection, live video walkthroughs, roommate interviews, and neighborhood orientation guides.

2. Service Packages & Pricing

Services are billed as a one-time flat fee based on the selected package:

  • 1 Apartment Inspection: €50
  • 2 Apartments Inspection: €80
  • 3 Apartments Inspection: €100

Optional Surcharges: Properties located outside the main Turin urban/university transit zone may incur an agreed travel surcharge prior to booking confirmation.

3. Booking, Scheduling & Cancellations

  • Payment: Full payment is required upfront before we contact landlords or schedule appointments.
  • Landlord Availability & Unavailability: We make reasonable efforts to schedule visits promptly. If a landlord rents out the property or cancels before our scout travels to the location, the Client may substitute another listing at no extra cost or request a full refund for the unvisited slot.
  • No-Show / Late Cancellation by Landlord at Door: If our scout arrives at the property at the confirmed time and the landlord/agent fails to appear, the visit counts as 50% of a viewing credit (allowing one replacement booking at half price) to cover transit time.
  • Client Rescheduling: Clients must notify us at least 12 hours before a scheduled viewing to reschedule without penalty.

4. Inspection Scope & Disclaimer of Liability

  • Visual & Non-Invasive Check: Our inspection covers visible features (window glazing, heating type, appliances, visible dampness/mold, water pressure, general condition, roommate presence, and surrounding neighborhood/transit routes). We do not perform invasive structural, electrical, or plumbing engineering audits.
  • Information Accuracy: Utility costs, condominium fees, and roommate details are reported based on on-site observation and statements made by the landlord, agent, or current tenants during the visit. RoomChecked Torino is not liable for misrepresentations made by third parties.
  • Contract Preparation Guidance: Any checklists or tips provided regarding Italian rental documents (Codice Fiscale, guarantor paperwork, contract types) are for general informational purposes only and do not constitute formal legal or tax advice. The Client assumes full responsibility for reviewing and signing their lease agreement and transferring any deposits directly to the verified landlord/agency.

5. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of Italy. Any disputes shall fall under the jurisdiction of the Court of Turin (Foro di Torino).
